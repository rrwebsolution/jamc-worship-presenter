## New scene sources (like OBS)
- **Display Capture:** shows a whole monitor of this computer live in a scene.
- **VLC Video (Playlist):** several videos as one source, played one after another, with **Loop** and **Shuffle**.
- **Scene:** shows another saved scene inside this one (for example a picture-in-picture). Edit that scene to change what shows.
- **Group:** keeps several layers together, so they move, show and hide as one. Removing the group can keep its layers where they are.
- **Audio Output Capture:** puts the PC's sound (other programs) into the recording and the livestream.
- **Application Audio Capture:** puts the sound of one program (a browser, Zoom…) into the recording and the livestream.

## Next / previous slide from any app
- **→ and ←** now move LiveCat's slides even while another app (WPS, PowerPoint, a browser…) is in front.
- Change the keys, or turn it off, in **Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts** ("Next / previous slide also work while any other app is in front").

## Projector screens that are not 16:9
- On a 16:10 projector, 4:3 TV or another screen, the picture now **fills the whole screen** by default.
- To keep the exact shape with black bars instead: **Settings → Output & OBS → Screen that is not 16:9 → Fit (black bars)**.

## LiveCat Remote
- **Fixed:** the Preview / Program pictures on the phone no longer fall seconds behind on slow Wi-Fi. They update about 3 times a second and always show the newest picture.
