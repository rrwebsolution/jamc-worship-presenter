Operator manual · LiveCat Multimedia

Running the screens for a worship service

For volunteers at the media desk. It explains how the app is organised, what to do before, during and after a service, and how to show lyrics, Bible verses, PowerPoint slides, videos and cameras on the projector and the livestream.

The process flow

Everything in the app follows one rule: you prepare in Preview, and the church sees Program. Nothing reaches the projector until you send it there.

1 · Library

Pick a song, verse, slide, video, image or scene. Clicking an item only shows it to you.

2 · Preview

The left monitor. Only you see it. Check it before it goes live.

TAKE

Space or the TAKE → button

3 · Program

The right monitor, marked LIVE. This is what everyone sees.

ProjectorTVsLivestreamRecordingOBS window

Lyrics and Bible go together on a click; PowerPoint on TAKE; videos and images do not. Videos and images use Preview and TAKE unless you untick Separate Videos or Separate Images. Clicking a lyric or verse shows it on Preview and Program at once. Clicking a PowerPoint slide selects it, and TAKE shows it on both. To have Preview first and TAKE afterwards, tick Separate Lyrics, Separate Bible or Separate Presentation in Settings → Live & Scenes.

A Sunday, step by step

The order most churches use. Do the "before" list at least 30 minutes before the service starts.

Before30–60 min early
  1. Connect the projector and TVs, then open the app.
  2. Open Settings → Projector. Check the projector display is right (it shows the screen's real name, such as "TCL SMART TV"). Press Identify Displays if you are unsure which is which.
  3. Press F5 to open the projector output. The top bar changes from OUTPUT OFF.
  4. Open the Service page and choose this week's service, or press New Service and add songs, verses, slides and videos with Add to Service.
  5. Pick the starting scene on the Scenes page (for example "Worship").
  6. Play one video briefly to check the sound reaches the speakers.
  7. If you livestream, open the Stream page and run Pre-stream Check.
Duringthe service
  1. Press ● START RECORDING and START STREAM when the service begins (both can run together).
  2. Work through the service list: Page Down moves to the next item.
  3. For songs, click each lyric slide as it is sung, or use → and ←.
  4. For anything else, put it in Preview, check it, then press Space to TAKE.
  5. Need a clean screen? B = black, W = hide the text, L = lyrics on/off.
Afterwhen people leave
  1. Stop the stream, then stop the recording. Wait for "Saving the MP4…" to finish.
  2. Press F5 to close the projector output.
  3. Close the app. Songs, services and settings save automatically.

The six pages

The buttons along the top switch between them. The Preview and Program monitors stay on screen on the Control and Service pages.

Control

Run the service from here. The Library list on the left has all content: Lyrics, Bible, Presentations, Images, Videos (with tabs for All, Local, YouTube and Backgrounds), Audio, Camera. Under "More": Custom Slides, Themes, Lower Thirds, Favorite Lyrics, All Media.

Service

This week's running order. Add items from the Library, drag to reorder, and step through with Page Up / Page Down.

Scenes

Screen layouts: camera, lyrics, logo and lower third arranged together. Edit, save and send them to Preview.

Output

Which screen shows Preview or Program, fullscreen or windowed, and the projector status.

Stream

Recording and livestreaming: platform, stream key, quality, microphones and the audio mixer.

Settings

Every option in one place, from fonts and transitions to cameras, recording and keyboard shortcuts.

Song lyrics

  1. Open Library → Lyrics and type the song name in the search box.
  2. Click the song. Its slides appear as cards.
  3. Click a lyric card. It goes live on Program straight away.
  4. Use → and ← for the next and previous slide.

New song: press ADD LYRICS and type or paste the words, or use the import options (a lyrics file, or lyrics found from YouTube). Lyrics are reviewed before saving and then work offline.

The look of lyrics (font, size, color, shadow) is set once for every song in Settings → Lyrics Typography. The preview beside it shows the result as you change it.

Bible verses

  1. Open Library → Bible and choose the version.
  2. Type a reference such as John 3:16, or pick the book and chapter.
  3. Click the verse. It shows on Preview and Program together. With Separate Bible on, it goes to Preview and Space sends it live.

Long verses split across slides by themselves. Add more versions with Settings → Manage Bible Versions….

PowerPoint

  1. Open Library → Presentations and press Import PowerPoint. Choose the .pptx or .ppt file.
  2. Click a slide to select it (white outline).
  3. Press Space to TAKE: it shows on Preview and Program together. With Separate Presentation on, double-click it to send it to Preview first.
  4. Press NEXT or → for the next slide. Preview and Program move together.

Slides look exactly as they do in PowerPoint. Their fonts are not changed by the app's typography settings.

Videos & images

Clicking a video or image shows it in the media panel only. Nothing changes on Preview or Program yet.

The four action buttons

ButtonWhat it does
SEND TO PREVIEWPuts it in Preview. TAKE sends it live.
ADD TO SERVICEAdds it to the service list. Nothing goes on screen.
FULLSCREEN VIDEO / FULLSCREEN IMAGEFills the whole screen. It goes to Preview first; TAKE puts it live.
TAKE AS BACKGROUNDMakes it the background behind lyrics and scenes. Greyed out for YouTube videos.

Playing controls

Under the video, and in the PROGRAM MEDIA row under the monitors: back 10 seconds, play / pause, forward 10 seconds, stop, a position bar, volume, mute and loop.

  • Preview's sound plays only through your monitor headphones, and only when PREVIEW AUDIO is on. The church never hears Preview.
  • Videos start with their own sound at 100%. Make all media quieter with the MEDIA fader in the MIXER.

YouTube: use Import from YouTube in the Videos library. YouTube videos need the internet while they play.

Backgrounds

  1. Open Library → Videos, choose the Backgrounds tab and click a video or image.
  2. Press TAKE AS BACKGROUND. It shows ✓ ACTIVE BACKGROUND.

The background controls below the monitors have back 10 s, Pause, forward 10 s, Restart, Change, and ON / OFF. Changing the background never changes the scene or the lyrics on top.

Scenes

A scene is a saved screen layout, for example "Camera + Lyrics" or "Worship". Scene cards are on the Scenes page and in the Scenes list beside the Library.

Editing a scene

  1. Right-click the card and choose Edit Scene…. Editing never changes Preview or Program.
  2. Add sources (camera, lyrics, image, video, presentation, text) with Add Source. For presentations, lyrics, images and videos choose From Library (follows whatever you send) or Specific File (always shows that one file).
  3. Press SAVE SCENE. The scene is stored but Preview and Program do not change; it shows only when you press TAKE. If you leave the Scenes page with unsaved changes, the app asks first.

Separate Layouts (in the Scenes list, and in Settings → Live & Scenes) lets the church TV keep one scene while the livestream keeps another. TAKE then changes only the content; TAKE SCENE → changes Program's layout.

Projectors & TVs

  • Screens are listed by their real name, for example "TCL SMART TV (Display 2)". Your own laptop screen is never covered by a right-click.
  • F5 opens or closes the projector. Esc closes a fullscreen projector.
  • The PREVIEW / PROGRAM switch above the monitors chooses what the projector shows. Program is normal.
  • If a cable comes loose, the output closes. It returns by itself when the screen is reconnected.

Record & livestream

  1. Open the Stream page. Choose the platform (Facebook, YouTube…) and press Change… to enter the stream key once.
  2. Choose the quality. 1080p 30 FPS suits most churches; use 720p on a slow computer or slow internet.
  3. Press Pre-stream Check, then START STREAM.
  4. Press ● START RECORDING to save an MP4 on the computer. Recording and streaming can run at the same time and stop separately.
  • Both use the Program picture and the same mixer (microphone, aux, media, master).
  • While a video or music plays, the microphones are muted in the recording and stream, so talking in the room is not mixed in. You can turn this off in Settings → Recording & Livestream.
  • The recording and stream take only the sound on Program. A browser, YouTube or anything else playing on the computer is not included. PREVIEW AUDIO and the Library preview stay silent until you stop.
  • Microphones still hear the room. If music plays on the speakers next to a microphone, it can be picked up that way, so keep other sound off during a service.
  • If the internet drops, the stream tries to reconnect and the recording keeps going.
  • Recording and streaming together roughly doubles the work for the computer. Use the Auto or Hardware encoder.

When something goes wrong

Nothing shows on the projector
  • Check the top bar. If it says OUTPUT OFF, press F5.
  • Check the screen is chosen in Settings → Projector, and press Identify Displays.
  • Check the projector switch above the monitors is on PROGRAM.
  • Check the screen is not on Black (B) or Blank (W).
A video has no sound in the church
  • Look at the PROGRAM MEDIA row: is it muted, or is the volume low?
  • Open the MIXER and check the MEDIA fader.
  • Check Settings → Audio uses the right speaker output.
  • Some videos have no audio track; the PROGRAM MEDIA row says so.
Space or the arrow keys do nothing

The cursor is probably still in a search box. Click anywhere outside it and try again.

The livestream stopped
  • It reconnects by itself for a short while. Watch the status on the Stream page.
  • The recording keeps going, so the service is still saved on the computer.
  • If it keeps failing, lower the quality to 720p and start the stream again.
A font is missing from the list

Install the font in Windows, then open Settings again; the list refreshes by itself. Or press Add Font… beside the font list and choose the TTF or OTF file.

A camera shows "CAMERA OFFLINE"
  • Check the USB cable. The camera comes back by itself when reconnected.
  • Several USB cameras on one port can run out of bandwidth. Move one to another USB port, or lower its resolution in Settings → Camera.
